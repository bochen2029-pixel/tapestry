## §2 · The four rules that are easy to get backwards

**1 · A hold is graded.** The verb that changes no row is most of what an autonomous system does, and if it goes ungraded the record flatters: you can only audit the actions, and the actions are the minority. A hold is **right** when the world closed the cell inside the horizon anyway, and **wrong** when the deadline passed while it was held. A hold on a cell with no deadline that nothing ever resolved is **ungradable** — not right **[M: `hold.*`]**.

The lie arm is the tempting one: a grader that scores an unresolved hold as `right` because nothing went wrong would report a perfect record and an `n_eff` a third larger than the truth. **Silence is not evidence** **[M: `hold.lie.silence_would_have_counted_as_success`]**.

**2 · An escalation the human rubber-stamps is WRONG.** §4.11 is explicit and counterintuitive: escalate is *right* when "the human's decision differed from the counterfactual" and *wrong* when "the human ratified the counterfactual, a rubber stamp". The escalation bought nothing — it spent a human on a decision the machine had already made correctly. The intuitive grader, which scores agreement as success, would report two right and none wrong on the same three decisions, and a class that escalated everything would look flawless while buying nothing **[M: `esc.lie.agreement_is_not_success`]**.

This is the only way escalation can be measured rather than assumed safe, and it is why §4.11 makes the seam record a `counterfactual_verb` on every entry.

**3 · `n_eff` is per verb, never pooled.** Falsifier 10's planted lie is the pooled count. The oracle builds a class with 200 graded holds and 3 graded acts: pooled, 203 clears a floor of 100 comfortably; per verb, the act side has three and the band is refused, naming the verb that failed **[M: `cal.lie.pooled_n_eff_would_have_licensed_it`]**. There is deliberately **no total field** in `VerbTally` — the surest way not to read a pooled count by accident is not to compute one **[SPEC]**.

The act side additionally needs **canary-source** outcomes: ten borrowed-source acts do not license acting, and the refusal says so by name **[M: `cal.borrowed_does_not_license_acting`]**.

**4 · A verb with no finite horizon cannot be graded, so its class is unlicensable.** Falsifier 13's lie is "fetch with no horizon" — the verb that quietly becomes the answer to everything because nothing can ever call it wrong. `Horizons::all_finite` refuses a zero or infinite horizon on any of the seven, and the class is unlicensable until it is fixed **[M: `hz.lie.*`, `fetch.lie.*`]**. Every fetch in the oracle gets a verdict: crossing a threshold is right, not crossing or hitting the depth cap is wrong.

## §3 · Five verdicts, because four would lose something

`reversed_late` is not `wrong`. An act reversed *inside* its horizon was a mistake; one reversed *after* it is the world changing its mind, and collapsing the two would blame the decision for the delay **[M: `act.lie.late_is_not_the_same_as_wrong`]**. `ungradable` is not `right`: it is the honest home for a decision whose outcome never arrived, excluded from `n_eff` and counted in the ungradable fraction, which §4.11 caps — because a class that mostly cannot be measured is a class that mostly cannot be licensed **[M: `cal.the_ungradable_ceiling_bites`]**.

## §4 · Time comes only from ticks

Every grade is produced either at outcome arrival or **at the first tick past the decision's horizon**. Nothing expires because a host clock moved: the fold's `now` is the stamp of the entry being folded, per §3.4. Two graders fed the same inputs produce the same digest; a repeated tick changes nothing **[M: `det.*`]**.

## §6 · Deltas against the blueprint

1. **`Horizons::all_finite` treats zero as no horizon**, not as an instant deadline. §3.3 names seven horizons and requires each verb to have one; a zero horizon would grade every decision at the moment it was made, which is the same evasion as an infinite one wearing the opposite mask.
2. **`VerbTally` exposes no total.** §4.11 says a band is calibrated only when every emittable verb clears the floor; not computing the pooled number is how that is enforced rather than remembered.
3. **The human's verb is fed to the fold separately from the fact that carries it.** §4.11 grades escalate against "the human's decision as a fact, against the recorded counterfactual verb" without saying how the decision reaches the fold. At R4 it will arrive on the tape; the interface here is the seam a real one will plug into.
